# BF16 RoPE rotate with on-die sin/cos generation — N28 synthesis result

On-die RoPE rotate unit, **BF16**, that **generates its own cos/sin from the
angle** (a LUT + linear-interpolation sincos unit) instead of taking them as
operand inputs. Head-to-head counterpart of `rotate_q_unit` (which is FP16 and
receives cos/sin on ports).

All numbers below are **MEASURED** — TSMC N28 Genus synthesis, SS / 0.72 V /
125 °C, 500 MHz, hierarchy-preserving (`run_syn_hier.tcl`). No estimates.

Reproduce: `cd syn && make rotbf16`
Reports: `syn/build_rotbf16/reports_rotbf16/rotate_q_bf16_*.rpt`

## What was built (`rtl/`)

| File | Role |
|---|---|
| `bf16_mult.sv`, `bf16_add.sv` | bfloat16 (1 sign / 8 exp / 7 mant, bias 127) mul/add, 1-cyc, RNE, FTZ |
| `sincos_bf16.sv` | 256-entry BF16 sine LUT + linear interpolation; `cos = sin(phase+¼turn)`; 3-cyc |
| `rotate_q_bf16.sv` | 8-pair (256-bit word) RoPE rotate: per pair, on-die `sincos_bf16` feeds the BF16 MAC `y[2i]=a·cos−b·sin`, `y[2i+1]=a·sin+b·cos` |

Interface: per pair it takes the operand pair `(a,b)` and the angle `phase`
(16-bit, `[0,2π)→[0,65536)`); it produces the rotated pair. The angle is the
only per-token input — the coefficients are made on the die.

## Reading the numbers

- **The on-die sin/cos generator costs ~3,018 µm² per lane** (measured). This is
  the whole point: with it, RoPE needs only the **angle** from upstream, not the
  cos/sin coefficient stream.
- The unit above instantiates **8 sincos generators** (one per pair) for full
  8-pairs/cycle throughput. If throughput can be traded down, a **single** shared
  `sincos_bf16` is 3,018 µm² and produces one pair/cycle.
- **BF16 shrinks the rotate MAC vs. FP16.** The BF16 MAC (8 pairs) is
  **17,787 µm²** here; the FP16 `rotate_q_unit` MAC is **28,029 µm²** (measured,
  `syn/AREA_BREAKDOWN.md`) — BF16's 7-bit mantissa gives ~37% smaller multipliers.
- The sincos generator sets the critical path (its 3-stage interpolation), but
  still closes 500 MHz at slack 0.

## Integrated into `fugue_logic_die` — full-die overhead vs AttAcc

`fugue_logic_die` now instantiates `rotate_q_bf16` in place of the old FP16
`rotate_q_unit`: the top no longer has `rope_cos`/`rope_sin` ports (512 bits of
streamed coefficients removed); it takes only `rope_x` (query) + `rope_phase`
(8 per-pair angles). Re-synthesized full-flatten at 500 MHz (`build_fugue_m2/`).

| Metric | AttAcc baseline | Fugue (BF16 M2 rotate) | Δ = overhead |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| **Total area** | 473,939 µm² | **523,585 µm²** | **+49,646 (+10.5%)** |
| — cell / net | 339,894 / 134,045 | 372,080 / 151,505 | +32,186 / +17,460 |
| Std-cell instances | 270,772 | 307,398 | +36,626 (+13.5%) |
| Total power (Genus est.) | 285.8 mW | 304.5 mW | +18.7 (+6.5%) |
| Timing | 500 MHz, slack 0 | 500 MHz, slack 0 | timing-neutral |

Versus the earlier FP16 / streamed-cos-sin Fugue (516,310 µm², +8.9% vs AttAcc),
moving sin/cos on-die adds only **+7,275 µm²** total despite the standalone 8-lane
sincos measuring ~24k µm² — because BF16 shrinks the rotate MAC (~28k→~17.8k) and
the 512-bit cos/sin input ports are gone. Net: the die takes only the angle
upstream (no 256 B/token cos/sin stream), at a die-level cost of +10.5% vs +8.9%.

### Where the RoPE cost sits — "Fugue 2" (RoPE on the GPU)

`fugue2_logic_die` (`build_fugue2/`, `filelist_fugue2.f`) drops the on-die rotate
entirely — the GPU applies RoPE — so the die adds only **TLB + diff_decoder**
(the "拼接"/merge) over AttAcc. Full-flatten, 500 MHz, slack 0:

| Config | Die contents | Total area | vs AttAcc | Power |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| AttAcc | GEMV+softmax+acc+ctrl | 473,939 µm² | — | 285.8 mW |
| **Fugue 2** (GPU RoPE) | +TLB +diff_decoder | **488,120 µm²** | **+14,181 (+3.0%)** | 292.4 mW |
| **Fugue** (on-die BF16 RoPE) | +TLB +diff +sincos rotate | 523,585 µm² | +49,646 (+10.5%) | 304.5 mW |

So the +10.5% Fugue overhead splits cleanly: **TLB + diff_decoder = +3.0%**
(14.2k µm²) and **on-die BF16 RoPE = +7.5%** (35.5k µm², = Fugue − Fugue 2). RoPE
is ~70% of the Fugue die overhead — the trade is +7.5% die area to avoid streaming
256 B/token of cos/sin from the GPU and doing RoPE preprocessing off-die.

## Notes

- `sincos_bf16.sv`'s LUT is generated by `scratchpad/gen_sincos.py`
  (`sin(2π·i/256)` rounded to BF16); verified against `math.sin` (e.g.
  idx 32 → 0.707, idx 64 → 1.0, idx 192 → −1.0).
- Functional exactness is not sign-off-verified (no testbench in this flow; see
  HANDOFF §9 item 3) — the design is architecturally complete and synthesizes
  clean/latch-free, which is what the area/timing/power result requires.
